product is official or endorsed by ListenShelf.

## Uses that do not require permission

You may:

- truthfully refer to ListenShelf, link to the official project, and discuss or
  review it;
- state that software is based on, derived from, or compatible with
  ListenShelf, provided the statement does not imply endorsement;
- describe a community-created skin as being "for ListenShelf" or "compatible
  with ListenShelf";
- show unaltered screenshots or branding when reporting on, teaching about, or
  accurately identifying ListenShelf; and
- redistribute an unmodified official build under the GPL while clearly
  identifying its source and not implying that you are the project owner.

## Uses that require written permission

Without prior written permission, you may not:

- use ListenShelf or a confusingly similar name as the name of another
  application, business, service, domain, or commercial offering;
- use the ListenShelf logo or application icon as your own product branding;
- present a modified build as an official ListenShelf release;
- imply sponsorship, partnership, certification, or endorsement by ListenShelf
  or Abel Papazian;
- label a third-party skin or product as "official"; or
- use the branding on merchandise or promotional products.

## Forks and modified builds

Forking and modifying the GPL-covered software is welcome under the terms of
the GPL. A publicly distributed modified build should:

1. use a distinct product name;
2. replace the ListenShelf logo, application icon, and other
   source-identifying branding;
3. state clearly that it is a modified project based on ListenShelf; and
4. preserve the copyright, license, attribution, and source-availability
   notices required by the GPL.

This policy is intended to prevent confusion about who publishes and supports a
product. It does not limit the rights granted to the software under the GPL or
prevent truthful references to ListenShelf.

[ListenShelf repository](https://github.com/apelpapa/ListenShelf-Audiobook-Player/issues).
